UAD 3.6

New UCDP submissions move to UAD 3.6 on November 2, 2026.

The appraisal job didn’t change. The report did: new data, new layout, and questions the old form never asked. You don’t have to work it out alone — we handle the research and the report, and Matt reads every file before it comes back to you.

Only a report first submitted to UCDP on or after November 2, 2026 has to be 3.6. A 2.6 already submitted stays 2.6, and revisions to it are accepted through May 3, 2027.
