UAD 3.6

New UCDP submissions move to UAD 3.6 on November 2, 2026.

The appraisal job didn’t change. The report did: new data, new layout, and questions the old form never asked. Only a report first submitted to UCDP on or after that date has to be 3.6. A 2.6 report already submitted stays 2.6, and revisions to it are accepted through May 3, 2027.

Have a 2.6 that won’t make it?

Your software won’t carry it over. We convert it into a native UAD 3.6 you review and sign: your data, your comps, your adjustments, moved into the new form. Send one you’re stuck with. Matt confirms the price and return time before you decide.

How conversion works

Need the whole report done?

You inspect and make the final calls. We complete the UAD 3.6 report behind you and return an editable working file for your review and signature.
