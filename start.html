What happens after you hit send

  1. 1.You get an order number and a private folder link, usually within business hours. Drop the packet in the folder, or reply to the acknowledgment with attachments.
  2. 2.You get a firm acceptance. Received is not accepted. I confirm the scope, the flat price, and a committed return time before production begins. If anything's missing, I tell you exactly what, and the clock waits for it.
  3. 3.You follow the report on its status page and get the draft back for your review by the committed time. You review, revise, sign, and deliver. If the draft isn't usable to you, you don't pay for it.

What to send, by assignment

Pick your scenario above, or browse here. Required is what production needs to start. Helpful is what makes the draft better and your review shorter.

Required · full interior

  • The order or engagement letter
  • Photos: front, rear, street, every room, mechanicals, any deficiency you noted
  • Sketch or measurement file, or your dimensions
  • Your condition and quality notes from the walk
  • Contract, if it's a purchase

Helpful

  • Voice memo from the drive home, I'll take it over typed notes any day
  • Updates and remodel history the owner mentioned
  • HOA info, if applicable
  • Anything odd: easements, additions, the neighbor's goats

Required · exterior-only

  • The order or engagement letter
  • Front and street photos
  • Your exterior condition notes

Helpful

  • Rear or side shots if you could get them
  • Anything you observed that records won't show

Required · desktop

  • The order or engagement letter
  • The data sources you're authorizing me to use
  • Floor plan or sketch source, if the scope requires one

Helpful

  • Prior listing or inspection material you're authorized to share
  • Your read on the neighborhood, if you know it

Required · rental income

  • Everything for the base scenario above
  • Current lease or leases, if any
  • Rent roll or rental history, if known

Helpful

  • What the owner says it rents for, so I can check it against the market
  • Furnished or unfurnished, and any included utilities

Required · legacy / non-GSE

  • The order, naming the exact form required
  • The client's engagement requirements or SLA, if they sent one
  • The inspection materials for your scenario

Helpful

  • A prior example the client accepted, if you have one
  • Any client-specific commentary requirements
