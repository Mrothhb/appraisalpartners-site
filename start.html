What to upload, by assignment

Pick your scenario above, or browse here. Required is what production needs to start. Helpful is what makes the report better and your review shorter.

Required · full interior

  • The order or engagement letter
  • Photos: front, rear, street, every room, mechanicals, any deficiency you noted
  • Sketch or measurement file, or your dimensions
  • Your condition and quality notes from the walk
  • Contract, if it's a purchase

Helpful

  • Voice memo from the drive home, I'll take it over typed notes any day
  • Updates and remodel history the owner mentioned
  • HOA info, if applicable
  • Anything odd: easements, additions, the neighbor's goats

Required · exterior-only

  • The order or engagement letter
  • Front and street photos
  • Your exterior condition notes

Helpful

  • Rear or side shots if you could get them
  • Anything you observed that records won't show

Required · desktop

  • The order or engagement letter
  • The data sources you're authorizing me to use
  • Floor plan or sketch source, if the scope requires one

Helpful

  • Prior listing or inspection material you're authorized to share
  • Your read on the neighborhood, if you know it

Required · rental income

  • Everything for the base scenario above
  • Current lease or leases, if any
  • Rent roll or rental history, if known

Helpful

  • What the owner says it rents for, so I can check it against the market
  • Furnished or unfurnished, and any included utilities

After the trial, if it went well

There is no contract to sign and nothing to join. The trial ends, you decide, and live work starts whenever you have a report worth sending.

  1. 1.Send your first live report through the thread, not this form. The form's consent says the file is a closed prior assignment, so live work starts with a reply in the order thread we already have open, or a call. You do not start over as a stranger.
  2. 2.Each live report still gets its own written acceptance. Scope, flat price, and committed return time before production begins, every time. That does not change once you are a regular. It is the thing that protects you.
  3. 3.Sending more than one at a time. Tell me what is coming and when. I hold the desk to what I can personally produce and review, so volume gets planned rather than assumed, and I will tell you what I can take before you send it.
  4. 4.Standing arrangements. If the desk becomes part of how your week runs, we set one up: your markets, your client mix and their restrictions, expected volume, the data path, software and delivery formats, and terms in writing. That conversation is worth having once, not once per file.

I keep the roster small on purpose, because the review is the product and it does not scale by wishing. If the desk is full when you want to start, I will say so directly instead of taking the work and running late.
