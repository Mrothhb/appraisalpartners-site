Questions we’d ask too.

Short answers. If yours is not here, email us and you’ll get a direct answer.

What do I upload?

Research & report · $149: Upload the order, engagement letter, contract if applicable, raw photos, sketch & field notes through the private checklist. You do not have to assemble the subject data or select comps first.

Your data & comps · $89: Upload the order, inspection material, subject data, comp set, supporting data & market direction.

Whose client is it?

Yours. We work behind you. We do not contact, invoice or solicit your client. You keep the engagement and appraisal fee.

Who is responsible for the appraisal?

You are. You make the final calls on scope, comps, adjustments, reconciliation & value. We return an editable working report for you to review, change and sign.

Is this allowed? How does disclosure work?

Only when your engagement, client, state & assignment program allow it. When our work is significant appraisal assistance, the report’s certification identifies Matthew Roth, his credential, that he did not inspect & what he did. We do not accept an assignment that cannot be handled that way.

Do you use AI?

Yes. We use software automation and AI-assisted tools in this work, and yes, that includes the drafting. Matt checks the source documents and reads every report himself. AI does not inspect, make the final comp or value decisions, or sign. What runs behind the desk →

What format comes back?

An editable working report file with the agreed photos & exhibits placed. We confirm the file format and delivery route before accepting the order.

How fast is it?

Fast turnaround is the point. We give you a return time before you say go. The clock starts when everything needed is in. If timing is at risk, we tell you before the due date.

How much can you take in a week?

The desk stays small on purpose, and capacity is checked before every acceptance, so a committed return time means something. If the week is full, we say so before you upload anything. Planning steady volume? Tell us what is coming and we’ll tell you straight what we can take.

Can I track the status of my appraisal report?

Yes. Every accepted report gets a private tracking link. See where it is at each step and choose email updates, text updates, both or status page only. View the full live tracking demo →

What if I do not like the comps?

Upload your selected set, ask to see candidates before we complete the grid, or review our choices with the report. You make the final selection. We will not chase a predetermined value.

What if something is wrong?

Give us one round of changes as one list. We correct our production errors at no charge. Client or underwriter conditions tied to our work are handled for 30 days. New scope is quoted before we touch it.

What does it cost?

$89 with your data & comps. $149 when we handle the research. These are desk fees paid by you, not appraisal fees billed to your AMC, lender, borrower or client. No subscription or minimum. The accepted price does not change later.

How can the fee be that low?

Matt built this workflow for his own reports first. It cuts re-keying, form filling & file chasing, so the hours go to research, analysis and the read-through. These are standing rates for defined scopes, not teaser prices.

What kind of past assignment can I use for the trial?

Use only a closed prior assignment you have actual authority and the original client’s explicit permission to share. If a lender or AMC prohibits sharing, that file is not eligible. A closed estate, divorce or other private-use assignment may work, but the assignment type alone does not make it shareable. Redaction alone is not permission.

Do you take legacy 1004/UAD 2.6 orders?

We never deliver a 2.6, but a 2.6 can come in. Draft your live order in the legacy form you know and we convert it to a native UAD 3.6 for your signature: how conversion works. A closed legacy assignment also makes a fine free trial; it comes back rebuilt as UAD 3.6 on a file you already know.

Can I use this only when reports stack up?

Yes. Upload one when you are buried in reports and none when you are not. There is no subscription or minimum. We take only what we can personally complete and review.

Where can you work?

With your data & comps, we can often help outside our licensed markets when the client, state & data rules permit it. Research & report requires authorized data access and market competence. DFW is ready now. Ask before uploading assignment materials.

What about confidential information?

Keep client and property information out of the opening form. Report files move through a private order folder; the tracking page never shows the borrower, property address or report data. We confirm the provider route before confidential files move, and you must be authorized to share them.

How do I start?

Submit the short request without confidential assignment files. If we can take it, you receive the AP order number, private tracker, restricted upload folder & checklist. Nothing enters production until we confirm the fee and return time and you approve the terms.
