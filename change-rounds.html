Change rounds

Why change rounds are limited.

Each order includes two change rounds. Here is why.

Why am I seeing this?

Your order has used both of its change rounds. The form is now closed for changes, and your report is delivered to your TOTAL folder automatically; you don’t need to approve it. To buy another round, email desk@aandap.com.

Why is it limited?

Two change rounds are included per order. Review the whole report and send your changes together.

Why two rounds?

We expect most corrections to be found in your first review pass, so please review the whole report thoroughly and send every change at once. We offer a second round as a grace round, because we understand things get missed. A third round shouldn’t be needed on a report that had a thorough first review.

What does another round cost?

$15 a round, by request to desk@aandap.com. You can also make any further changes yourself in your TOTAL report; it’s your report.

What if it’s our mistake?

We fix our mistakes free, at any time, and they never use a round. A re-send with nothing changed doesn’t count either. The full wording is in the Software Terms (A&AP Auto-pilot) and the Service Terms (partial and full).

What isn’t part of a round?

On orders accepted under the November 2, 2026 terms or later, replacing comps you selected and supplied, or heavy rework because required information you sent was missing or wrong, is quoted separately. We tell you the work and the total fee first, and nothing starts or is charged without your written approval. It uses no round and adds no $15 round fee. Corrections to work we agreed to do, and your client’s revision requests within the same assignment, stay free; when it’s unclear whose it is, it’s ours. You can decline the quote, approve delivery and edit in TOTAL. Earlier orders keep their accepted terms.
