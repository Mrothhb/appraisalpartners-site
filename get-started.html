Step by step

  1. 1.Write us at mrr@appraisalpartners.net with the property address in the subject line and the order basics: county, scope (full, hybrid, or desktop), client name as it should read on the report, intended use, effective date, and when you need it. Prefer the phone? The number comes with our first reply.
  2. 2.You get an answer within 30 minutes during desk hours (7am–10pm Central, seven days): accepted with a quoted delivery time, exactly what's missing, or "needs a closer look" with a decision time. Never silence. Accepted orders get a secure upload link — please don't email assignment files.
  3. 3.Upload the packet — the checklist is short: inspection results (condition, quality, room count, GLA, anything unusual — typed notes or voice memos both work), sketch with GLA, photos in obvious order, the contract if it's a purchase, your comps with MLS sheets plus your adjustments — or say "propose adjustments" and we draft them with their data basis for your review — market data, and HOA/PUD info if any. (That's the standard lane. Onboarded for the full appraisal? Just the order, photos, sketch, and notes — we build the rest.)
  4. 4.Reply ACCEPTED to the acceptance email — it references the Service Terms, and that reply is what starts production. Paid orders turn next-day — the committed time is quoted at acceptance — and if anything ever threatens that clock, you hear from us in advance, not after. Your free first file runs in a scheduled slot behind paid work, typically one to two days, with its committed time quoted the same way. Review it, change anything, sign and deliver like always.
