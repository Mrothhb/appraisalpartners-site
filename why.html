Why we're doing this

← back to the front page

The honest answer, from the appraiser who runs the desk.

After twenty years in appraisal, I know where I do my best work: analysis, report construction, and clear writing. I didn't want to build another inspection practice or chase lender volume. I wanted to work directly with other appraisers — they're my people, and this desk answers to their judgment and nobody else's.

You keep the inspection, the comp selection, the hunt for good data, the client relationships, and the final judgment. We take the production work that eats the evening — data entry, report assembly, commentary, and consistency checks — and send back a draft built for your review, written by appraisers who read a file the way you do. Focus on what you want to focus on. Let us handle the rest.

This is a wonderful vocation when it's given the time, space, and intention to be done right — and in a rush-everything world, the report writing is where that time goes to die. UAD 3.6 makes the support especially useful now: the form is changing, but your pipeline doesn't have to stop while you learn it. Time back, without surrendering an inch of control over your work.

I still love this work. I built the desk so the appraisers I work with have room to love theirs too.

We love our work — so you can love yours again.

— Matt Roth · Certified Residential, TX & CO
