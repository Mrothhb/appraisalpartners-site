Legacy to UAD 3.6

Bring your existing report into UAD 3.6.

a la mode says it plainly: a 2.6 report cannot be converted to UAD 3.6, only about 6% of the fields line up, and they are not building a converter. Their answer is start over and carry the data by hand. We automated that for our own files. This is that tool, opened up.

UCDP requires UAD 3.6 for any report first submitted on or after November 2, 2026. A 2.6 report already submitted stays 2.6, and revisions to it are accepted through May 3, 2027.

How it works.

Your name, email and license. You get a private link to upload your report.
2. We convert it
We transfer the information your report contains and flag what the new form still needs. An appraiser reads it.
3. You get it back
An editable 3.6.
