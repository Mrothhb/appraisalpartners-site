Who does what

You decide

  • Inspection findings
  • Comparable selection
  • Adjustment methodology and amounts
  • Analyses, conclusions, value

We produce

  • Data entry and photo/sketch assembly
  • Source-based drafting and commentary
  • Proposed adjustments with their data basis, for your approval — nothing stands without your sign-off
  • Exhibits, formatting, consistency checks

We never

  • Sign or deliver
  • Communicate with your client
  • Silently change appraisal judgment
  • Touch your MLS login

If our drafting has an error, we fix it free and fast, and it's on us. The appraisal itself — analyses, conclusions, value — is yours; the agreement puts that split in plain English.

The disclosure, exactly as it appears

Every draft carries the USPAP SR 2-3 assistance disclosure, naming a person, not a company. This is the language, as placed in every draft:

Matthew Roth, Texas Certified Residential Appraiser #1361115-CR, provided significant real property appraisal assistance to the appraiser signing this certification. The extent of that assistance was: entry of property and market data into the report software; research and assembly of comparable data for the appraiser's review and selection; drafting of the report, including the sales grid and commentary, for the appraiser's review; and quality checks against source documents. All analyses, opinions, and conclusions — including comparable selection, all adjustments, and the opinion of value — are those of the signing appraiser, who reviewed and approved the report in its entirety. Matthew Roth did not inspect the subject property.

The extent list is trimmed to what we actually did on your assignment, and the credential recited matches the subject's state — Colorado assignments cite CO #CR200002387. You may edit the wording to fit your report; removing it ends our work together. Your client sees a named, plainly described service — the same thing this page tells you, and the same thing the work sample shows.

Current fit

UAD 3.6 conventional and private work. We don't take FHA or VA panel assignments — those panels carry roster and handbook requirements we're not set up to serve yet — and Georgia bars out-of-state appraisal assistance, so we sit that state out. Full Research + Draft runs Texas and Colorado by default; other markets open case by case.

The acceptance promise

Orders are reviewed as they come in. Within 30 minutes during desk hours (7am–10pm Central, seven days) you get one of three answers: accepted and the clock's running; what's missing; or "needs a closer look — answer within two hours." Never silence. How two people keep that promise seven days a week: the acknowledgment and first-pass triage run on the desk's systems, so the response window never depends on a human staring at an inbox — the licensed judgment happens at acceptance and review. An assignment is accepted only when the packet is confirmed complete, authorized, and assigned an open production slot — the delivery time is quoted at acceptance. Accepted orders get a secure upload link for assignment files (or your client's approved channel); plain email stays for contact and questions. Paid live assignments outrank demonstrations; rush only when expressly confirmed. The clock starts at acceptance, not when you hit send.

Matt's an appraiser who's also an engineer, and he built the desk's production stack himself, for this exact job. It does the data entry, grid assembly, formatting, and error checks against the source documents, over and over, without getting tired at 11pm. No magic, no shortcuts — an appraiser who got tired of typing and knew how to fix it.

The free First-File 3.6 Conversion

One complimentary conversion per firm: see your own workflow translated into the new format using a past file — with your client's written authorization — or a training file we provide. You get the native working file back, a missing-information memo, and a short call with Matt. Scheduled slots, subject to availability; training and evaluation only; paid production always comes first — demonstrations typically come back within one to two days of acceptance, not the next-day paid turnaround. (Why the authorization requirement? Assignment results are confidential to your client under USPAP — we built the offer around that instead of ignoring it.)

Full Research + Draft

After separate onboarding and agreement, we can perform agreed desk-based data collection, build the MLS and public-record research packet, develop proposed market and comparable analysis, and prepare the report data and narrative. You complete the required inspection and retain independent responsibility for scope, appraisal judgments, reconciliation, value, certification, signature, and delivery — everything we produce reaches you as a proposal for your review, disclosed like all our assistance. Availability is market-specific — licensing, client requirements, and authorized MLS and data access, meaning our own memberships joined per market during onboarding — Texas and Colorado by default, other markets case by case; setup typically takes one to three weeks after eligibility and access are confirmed. Every arrangement starts with onboarding and a fit check under its own clear agreement. We never share MLS credentials, in either direction. If that's the lane you want, ask before your first order.

Where your data goes

Your packet is used for your assignment and nothing else. No outside human subcontractors — the only people in your file are the two of us; production runs on our own systems and standard commercial software and cloud services under their confidentiality terms. Nothing is sold, reused for another customer, or used to train anyone's models. The report, the working file, and the workfile copy are yours; we keep our own assistance record confidentially, as USPAP expects, and delete anything beyond it on written request. If your client requires secure transfer, tell us your channel and we'll use it. NDA available on request.

One check that saves everyone a headache: read your engagement letter first — some letters restrict outside assistance. Before any order is accepted, you confirm the engagement and client permit the requested assistance; it's part of intake, not an afterthought.
