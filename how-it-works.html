Why I am offering this

The desk side is the part of appraisal I have always gravitated toward.

I have spent twenty years in residential appraisal. I like the research, the analysis, and the report writing itself. Over time, I have developed repeatable ways to organize source material, keep the report consistent, and catch omissions.

I want a few stable working relationships where the division of work is clear, the signing appraiser stays in control, and the client receives credible work. UAD 3.6 makes that help useful now, but it is not a reason to cut corners or hide who did what. Capacity and delivery timing are agreed before the first file and kept at a level I can personally review.

Who this can help

Appraisers and appraisal firms

  • You want to keep inspecting the property, serving the client, and making the final judgment
  • You need recurring capacity without hiring and training another employee
  • You need the report and support file built, not just fields typed
  • You will independently review the work and disclose significant assistance
  • You want to keep production moving through UAD 3.6

AMCs and lenders

  • Your program permits named significant appraisal assistance
  • You want to test a limited arrangement with participating signing appraisers
  • You need a clear division of responsibility and documented data handling
  • You will use your normal vendor approval and written terms

Not a fit: anonymous ghostwriting, shared credentials, co-signing, or any report the signing appraiser will not independently review.

The responsibility line

The signing appraiser retains

  • Client and engagement relationship
  • Inspection and inspection findings
  • Scope and final comparable selection
  • Final adjustments and appraisal judgments
  • Reconciliation and opinion of value
  • Certification, signature, and delivery

Appraisal Partners produces

  • Authorized MLS and public-record research
  • Comparable candidates and proposed analyses
  • Report data and narrative
  • Maps, exhibits, and organized source material
  • Consistency and completion checks
  • An editable draft report and supporting file

A narrower draft-from-your-research scope can also be arranged when your office already has the comps, adjustments, and market data assembled.

How a file moves

  1. 1.We establish the relationship first. Onboarding covers your markets, client mix, engagement restrictions, expected volume, authorized data access, software and deliverables, schedule, and written terms.
  2. 2.You forward the order and engagement letter. After the inspection, you send the photos, sketch, and written or voice notes through the agreed secure channel.
  3. 3.I produce the desk side of the assignment. I assemble the support, research the agreed sources, develop comparable candidates and proposed analyses, complete the report, and run the file through quality checks.
  4. 4.You review it as the appraiser. Accept, change, or reject anything necessary. You make the final appraisal judgments, sign, and deliver to your client.

What you receive

The working arrangement defines the exact package. For a typical supported assignment, that means an editable working file in the agreed software format, applicable PDF and UAD output, organized supporting materials, and a short record of assumptions or unresolved items that deserve attention during review.

I fill the report as completely as the evidence allows and flag anything you need to verify or decide.

Markets and current boundaries

Designed for

  • Established residential appraisers and firms
  • AMC or lender arrangements that permit named assistance
  • Recurring UAD 3.6 production needs

Current boundaries

  • Texas and Colorado are the starting markets
  • Other markets require client, licensing, and data-access review
  • Current scope is conventional and private residential work
  • No shared MLS credentials, in either direction

Market setup is deliberate. Client permission, licensing, market competence, and authorized MLS and data access are confirmed before production begins; a new market may take one to three weeks to establish.

Disclosure, data, and trust

When my work constitutes significant real property appraisal assistance, the certification identifies me and describes the assistance provided. Client, jurisdiction, and licensing requirements are confirmed before an assignment is accepted. I do not remove a required disclosure.

Files move through an agreed secure channel. No outside human subcontractors receive the assignment, no client is solicited, no MLS login is shared, and assignment data is never sold.

I use software automation and AI-assisted tools for clerical work, research organization, drafting, and quality checks. I direct and review the production file; the signing appraiser independently reviews and owns the appraisal judgments. Before live work begins, we identify the software and service providers expected to handle assignment data, along with their available storage, retention, and data-use terms, so you and your client can approve the setup.
