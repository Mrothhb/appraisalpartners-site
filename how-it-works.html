The handoff, start to finish.

  1. 01Submit the short request. No confidential assignment files in the opening form.
  2. 02Open the private intake. You get an AP order number, private tracker, restricted upload folder & order-type checklist.
  3. 03Upload the checklist items. The checklist changes with the assignment and the amount of research you want us to handle.
  4. 04We review the package. We check permission, completeness, conflicts, data access, UAD 3.6 fit & capacity.
  5. 05Approve the exact terms. Scope, desk fee & return time are in writing. Uploading alone never starts production.
  6. 06We complete the report. You review & sign. Track it as it moves, review the editable report, change anything you want & make every final appraisal decision.

A complete standard order might come in Thursday evening and be back Friday afternoon. Your actual return time is confirmed before we accept it.
