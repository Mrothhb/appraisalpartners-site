A few straight answers

Who makes the appraisal decisions?

You do. You inspect, decide, review, sign and deliver to your client. We prepare the report for you. The desk fee is for production assistance.

What does the software do?

Software does the pulls, assembly and checks. Claude and GPT help with research and drafting. Matt reviews the report; you decide and sign. Your files are never used for training.

Does zero E&O errors mean the appraisal is correct?

It means the report passes the software’s E&O checks. It does not establish appraisal correctness or lender acceptance. Your final review still matters.

When does the clock start?

When the files are complete and you tap READY on the order form. The form shows your quote and ready day before work starts.

What if the 2.6 is missing new information?

We flag the 3.6 questions the old report cannot answer. You supply or confirm the missing facts. We don’t fill gaps with guesses.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Request them on the order form. Revisions within the agreed scope are included; new scope is quoted before we proceed.

How do I send the files?

Start with the setup request. Matt gives you a private order folder. Keep assignment files out of the public form and ordinary signup notes.

Is there a subscription or minimum?

No. Send one report when you need help. We confirm capacity and timing before accepting it.

Can I try a closed file first?

Yes, a free first look at a closed file you have actual authority and client permission to share. Select that option on Start.

Where does the finished file go?

After your approval, the native, editable report goes into your own TOTAL folder. Open it, review the appraisal and sign when you’re satisfied.
