A&AP Auto-pilot · A visual tour

From your file to your final say.

One place to follow your report, review your calls and ask for changes.

Demo screenshots with fictional details. The sample report is not an appraisal.

  1. 01

    Send your file.

    Save your inspection in TOTAL. Drop the file, photos, sketch and comps in your folder. MLS sheets and the market export go in too. A finished 2.6 works the same way.

  2. 02

    Your orders, at a glance.

    See your orders and what needs your review.

  3. 03

    Press Start.

    One button on your order page. From here the report is on the line. Nothing more for you to type.

  4. 04

    Watch it move, or don't.

    Your order page shows each stage as it's done. Check it from the truck, or ignore it until it's ready. Either way, you get a note when it's finished.

  5. 05

    Review.

    Open the finished report in TOTAL. Grid, adjustments, comments in your words, addenda, photo pages. Every UAD 3.6 field checked. TOTAL's error check at zero. Read it like your own work, because once you sign, it is.

    TOTAL view

    PDF

    Sanitized UAD 3.6 Work Sample

    Read the complete 26-page sample report. Identifying property and client details have been removed.

    Open full screen ↗Download PDF (9.5 MB)
  6. 06

    Your calls stay yours.

    Open a section. Keep it, change it or see why it was proposed.

  7. 07

    Ask for changes.

    Don't like a comp, an adjustment or a sentence? Say so on your order page and it comes back revised. Or just change it yourself in TOTAL. Either way is fine.

  8. 08

    Approve.

    When it reads the way you'd write it, tap Approve.

    Your approval

    Delivered.

    The finished report lands in your TOTAL folder. Sign it and send it to your client the way you always have. Your client hears from you, never from us.

Never ours: inspecting, signing, your client. Our part is named in your certification, as USPAP requires. Files are never used to train AI.
