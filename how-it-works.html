We do it all: you send the comps. We do the rest.

  1. You inspect and send the comps01

    Save your inspection in TOTAL. Add your comps, MLS sheets, market export and all the data. We pick up your TOTAL file, photos and sketch from your shared folder. Include your field notes, the client order and the agreed supporting material.

    Have a finished 2.6 instead? Send that. It comes back as a native 3.6, included. Your order form shows the quote and ready day up front, before we start.

  2. We complete everything02

    We complete every part around your comps: the grid, supported adjustments, every comment in your voice and the value conclusion, ready for your review. Every page, map, photo and addendum, checked.

    Every UAD 3.6 field checked. TOTAL's error check at zero.

  3. You review on your form03

    Your form holds the quote, ready day and status. Review the report, approve it or request a change.

    Your analysis, your value. Change any comp, adjustment or comment before you sign. Our help is named and described in your report.

  4. Back in TOTAL to sign04

    The report lands in your TOTAL folder to sign. You make every final appraisal decision, sign and deliver to your client.
