This is software. You inspect, pick the comparables, and make every appraisal decision on the order's form and in TOTAL. In these terms, "we" and "A&AP" mean M ROTH SOFTWARE CONSULTING LLC. 1. What the software does, and what it never does

The software fills the facts and the arithmetic: property data, market data, exhibits, your comparables, your adjustments applied to your numbers, and draft text built from your inspection and your sources. It enters your documented decisions; it never chooses comparables, ratings, rates, weights, assumptions, repair costs, rents, values, or other appraisal conclusions, or preselects your answer. Your later approval of a conclusion chosen here does not make it your supplied decision. A blank stays blank and comes back to you as a question. Your value is entered exactly as you give it.

You are the appraiser of record. You inspect, decide, review every page, sign, and deliver. What the software assembles is information for you to evaluate, not an opinion. A&AP and Matthew Roth do not develop or professionally review the appraisal, advise on any appraisal call, or contact your client in this lane. They are not identified as appraisal assistants when they provided no such assistance. Keep truthful technology disclosures and identification of your actual assistants in the report. A&AP is not an appraiser and not an appraisal management company. FHA, VA, and USDA orders are not accepted in this lane.

2. Support

Support covers the software: how to use the form, your files and data, errors, and anything the build got wrong against your files or answers. Those corrections are free and take priority.

A question about the report is answered by what the report says and where each number came from, never by what it should say. If you want an appraiser to review the report or weigh in on a call, that is a different service: we first obtain your acceptance of the reviewed lane's scope, provider, price, and Service Terms, where state law and your client permit it. We do not do that review inside a self-service order, on a call or otherwise.

3. Your files, our providers, and confidentiality

Your report and workfile are yours. When the report comes back, its Workfile holds the support bundle (sources, market data, tables, photos, records) and a dated copy of your form answers at every READY, NEEDS CHANGE, and DONE. We keep the confidential work record, support, instructions, permissions, and accepted terms and history for at least five years after preparation, longer when legally required. We provide a copy within five business days of request, even after service ends or the form closes. Keep your final signed report and required workfile. Third-party source material remains subject to its owners' rights and permitted uses.

Here is the setup, so you know it before your first order:

  • Where the files live. Your order folder is in Google Drive under our Google Workspace business account. The report itself is a TOTAL file from a la mode, the software your report already runs in.
  • Who processes them. AI providers process order material to build the draft. We may change providers as tools improve. Paid accounts run under standard published terms, which may be consumer terms; we do not promise a negotiated data agreement.
  • Data use. Optional model training stays off. We do not submit your material for training, feedback, unrelated products, or another customer's work. Provider retention, safety-review, and legal-disclosure terms still apply. We read the order's documents, our reference material, and your customer profile as needed for the order, support, security, and records. Send only needed personal information; we minimize and protect it.
  • Changes. We give written notice before a material provider or data-handling change and obtain any required new permission before using it for your order.

We use your order's information only for the order, support, security, and required records. We protect it with reasonable safeguards and do not sell it or solicit your client. Sharing is limited to authorized people and providers for this work, your permission, or legal requirements; those we engage must follow appropriate confidentiality and safeguards. We notify you without undue delay of unauthorized access affecting your information, provide updates, and make legally required notices. Our nonpublic software and process remain ours.

4. Price, changes, and revisions

The price is per order, as quoted when the order is confirmed, and does not change. Each order has its own invoice with a PayPal payment link. Card payments carry a 3% processing fee only where applicable law and payment-provider rules permit; any fee and the total are shown before READY. Payment is due within 15 days unless the order's form states an earlier payment-before-release condition. When the form says payment is due, your file follows payment. There is no subscription, minimum, or long-term commitment. You may cancel before production starts at no charge. After it starts, we may charge only for completed work, never more than the accepted fee.

Request changes through the form. Corrections and revisions within the agreed assignment are free and take priority, including one extra comp each time a revision comes back. Additional comps or materially new work are quoted and accepted before they begin. You supply any new appraisal decisions. If we cannot correct an item expressly included in the scope, that order's fee is waived or refunded if already paid.

The form sets this order's agreed ready day. Missing files, changed instructions, or service interruptions may require a new day, which we explain and agree with you on the form. We do not guarantee a loan closing.
