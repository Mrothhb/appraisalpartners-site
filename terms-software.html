This is software, and you are the appraiser. You inspect, you choose, and you sign. On each order you pick one of two options. Conversion: you send a finished report on the prior form, and the software carries your decisions into the current UAD 3.6 form. Self-service: you send your inspection and data, and the software builds the report, fills the facts and arithmetic, and proposes each open appraisal call with the support it found, for you to confirm or change, section by section, before the report comes back to your folder. A proposal is information for your decision. It becomes part of your appraisal only when you confirm or change it on the order's form and sign the report.

In these terms, "we" and "A&AP" mean M ROTH SOFTWARE CONSULTING LLC, a Texas company. These terms apply to a self-service or conversion order accepted with that company. No person at A&AP develops the appraisal, reviews it as an appraiser, advises on any appraisal call, or is named in your report. If you want an appraiser in the reviewer seat, that is the separate A&AP Service Terms, run by Appraisal Partners of Texas, Inc., where the law of the property's state permits it.

1. What the software does, and what it never does

Conversion. The software carries the decisions in your finished report, including your comparables, ratings, adjustments, comments and value, into the current form without changing them, and shows you each place where the new form asks for something your report did not hold. Those come back to you as proposals or questions. Your decisions are carried, not re-made.

Self-service. The software fills the facts and the arithmetic from your inspection, your files, public records and market data under your authorized access, and drafts the report text. For every appraisal call your files do not already settle, including comparable selection and weight, quality and condition ratings, adjustment rates, time and concession adjustments, site value, premise, exposure time and the value conclusion, it drafts a proposal with the evidence it used and your usual rates from your profile where you have given them. The software does not make the call for you. A proposal is provisional information generated by software, with no person's judgment behind it. It is not an appraisal, an appraisal review, or an opinion of value by A&AP or anyone at A&AP. It can be wrong, incomplete or inconsistent. You must evaluate each proposal against your own inspection, knowledge and sources, and confirm it or change it. Only then is it your decision.

The software never signs or certifies for you, never contacts your client, borrower or lender, and never changes a result to reach a requested number. No one at A&AP, including Matthew Roth, develops or professionally reviews the appraisal, advises on any call, or supervises your work in this lane. Because no person at A&AP gives appraisal assistance, no A&AP person, license or company is named in your report, and the certification names only the people you say helped you. Name every person who actually gave you significant appraisal assistance; say no one only when that is true. The report carries a short statement, drafted for you and adopted by your Approve and signature, that you used AI-assisted report-preparation software under your direction and reviewed the result. Keep that statement and any technology disclosure your client requires; edit it if it is not true for your order. A&AP is not an appraiser, an appraisal firm, or an appraisal management company.

Programs and states. FHA, VA and USDA orders are not accepted in this lane. The software is available for property in any state where an appraiser may use report-preparation software and make every appraisal decision personally. We do not accept an order where your state, your client or a program requires a credentialed person on our side to develop, review or supervise the work, because no such person is in this lane. In Illinois, no one at A&AP holds an Illinois credential, and no appraisal help of any kind is available; the form asks for the Illinois AMC fee disclosure where it applies. In Missouri, Kansas and Tennessee, where the law lets a signing appraiser use supervised assistants, nothing here is a supervised assistant: the software is a tool under your control. These terms do not create a licensing exemption or override any state, client or program requirement. You are responsible for knowing and meeting your own state's rules on technology, disclosure and record keeping.

2. Your review, your calls, and delivery

When the build is done, your form shows the report pages, the PDF, and a list of every proposal marked for your call, section by section. You confirm or change each one. A change is entered as you give it, and the report is rebuilt or edited to match, with the arithmetic and text that follow from it; where a change conflicts with another of your calls, we flag it rather than silently changing your decision. Approve and delivery stay locked until every proposal is confirmed or changed. Approving means "deliver my report": the report file goes to your shared folder, and you review every page, sign and deliver it to your client yourself.

You may ask us, before you approve, to show where a number came from. We answer with what the report says and the sources it cites, never with what it should say. If you want an appraiser to review the report or weigh in on a call, that is a different service under the Service Terms, offered only where the property's state and your client permit it and only after you accept its scope, provider and price. We do not do that review inside a self-service order, on a call or otherwise.

3. Support, corrections, and revisions

Support covers the software: the form, your files, errors, and anything the build got wrong against your files, your confirmed calls or its own cited sources. Our mistakes are ours to fix, free, at any time, and they take priority. Request every change through the form so it stays in the order record.

Once the report is in your folder, a change is a revision. Revisions within the same assignment are free: a corrected fact, a changed call, a different comparable from your market data, a client's revision request on the report you signed. We change a signed report only on your revision request, after its signatures are removed; the revised report comes back unsigned for you to review and sign again. Keep your own copy of the report you signed and delivered. A new effective date, a new inspection, a different property or client, a program change, or a report type the order did not cover is a new order at the price then on file. If we cannot correct an item within the order's scope, that order's fee is waived or refunded.

4. Your files, our providers, and confidentiality

Your report and workfile are yours. When the report comes back, its Workfile holds the support bundle (sources, market data, tables, photos, records), the proposal list with its evidence, and a copy of your order form with its answers and history. We keep the confidential order record (files received, support, instructions, permissions, proposals, your confirmations, accepted terms and history) for at least five years after delivery, longer when the law requires. We provide a copy on request, even after service ends. At your request we delete the other copies of your order files that we control once that record is kept; a deletion request does not erase that record, a legal hold, or copies a provider keeps under its own retention terms. Keep your own signed report and required workfile. Third-party source material stays subject to its owners' rights.

Here is the setup, so you know it before your first order:

  • Where the files live. Your order folder is in Google Drive under our Google Workspace business account, with two-step sign-in and access limited to the people and systems working your order. The report itself is a TOTAL file from a la mode, the software your report already runs in.
  • Who processes them. Order material is processed by AI providers, today Anthropic (Claude) and OpenAI (GPT), and by Google through Workspace. We may change providers as tools improve and will keep a current list available on request. Paid accounts run under each provider's standard published terms, which may be consumer terms; we do not promise a negotiated data agreement with any provider.
  • Data use. Model training is off on every account that touches your material. We do not submit your material for training, feedback, unrelated products, or another customer's work. Each provider's own retention, safety-review and legal-disclosure terms still apply to what passes through it. We read the order's documents, our reference material and your customer profile as needed for the order, support, security and records.
  • Less is safer. Send only what the report needs. Do not send Social Security numbers, bank or card numbers, or borrower credit information; we never need them, and we remove them if we find them.
  • Lender and AMC duties. Under the federal Safeguards Rule an appraiser is a financial institution and we are your service provider for the customer information in an order. We use it only for your order, support, security and required records, under the safeguards described here, and we will confirm those safeguards in writing if your client requires it. If your client's terms require more, send them before Start and we tell you whether we can meet them.
  • Changes. We give written notice before a material provider or data-handling change and obtain any required new permission before using it for your order.

We protect your information with reasonable safeguards, do not sell it, and do not solicit your client. Sharing is limited to authorized people and providers for this work, your permission, or legal requirements; those we engage must follow appropriate confidentiality and safeguards. We notify you without undue delay of unauthorized access affecting your information, give updates, and make legally required notices. Our nonpublic software, prompts, rules and process remain ours; you may not copy, extract or reverse engineer them, and feedback you give us may be used to improve the software without naming you or your orders.
