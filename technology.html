How our system works

Most AI you've been shown
is a chat window.

Ours is a production pipeline, not a chat window. Software agents research the subject and market, gather the evidence, draft the analysis and challenge the draft. Fixed tools assemble the native report. The signing appraiser makes every decision.

The decision boundary stays human.

The appraisal thinking the assignment requires does not go away; it moves to the front, where it belongs. Our agents prepare and challenge the work. Fixed software assembles and checks it. An internal appraisal reviewer reads the draft against its sources. The signing appraiser retains the inspection, final appraisal judgment, value, certification, signature, and delivery. Data handling and required assistance disclosures are agreed before production.
