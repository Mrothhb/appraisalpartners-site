Report Service Terms

Version dated September 19, 2026. Accepted by email. No signature page.

The arrangement is simple: you keep the client, the assignment, the appraisal decisions, and the signature. I complete only the desk work we agree to and return an unsigned draft for your review. I personally review every report before it comes back to you.

In these terms, "I" and "Appraisal Partners" mean Appraisal Partners of Texas, Inc., acting through Matthew Roth.

Agree once by email

I send these terms as a PDF. The owner or an authorized representative accepts them by replying from a business email:

AGREED - I accept the Appraisal Partners Report Service Terms dated September 19, 2026 for [name or business].

That reply is the entire agreement step. There is no form or signing service.

For each order:

  1. Email me the client's order: the engagement letter, the order, the contract if there is one, and anything the client provided.
  2. I reply in that thread with the partial-desk and full-desk rates and the return time.
  3. You reply that it works and which one you want. Any plain yes is the acceptance for that order, and nothing starts without it.
  4. I send the link to the assignment's private shared Drive folder.
  5. After the inspection, add the photos, sketch, notes, mobile report file, and other agreed materials to the folder.
  6. The finished unsigned report lands in the same folder for your review.

1. The work

Each assignment states the desk work I will perform. When that work is significant real property appraisal assistance, I work as an independent subcontractor under the signing appraiser's direction, where permitted.

I do not take the assignment from your client, inspect the property, sign the report, or deliver it to the client. The signing appraiser inspects, independently reviews the draft, makes the final appraisal decisions, signs, and delivers it.

2. Files, tools, and disclosure

By approving an order or adding files to its folder, you confirm that I am qualified for the agreed assistance and that the client, engagement, and applicable requirements permit it and the file sharing involved.

I may use software automation and AI-assisted tools. Before the first assignment, I tell you which providers receive the file, the type of account used, and the relevant data-use and retention settings. I tell you in writing before materially changing that setup. I personally review the draft against the available sources. When disclosure is required, the report accurately identifies my assistance and states that I did not inspect.

I use assignment information only to review your request, perform accepted work, and keep required records. I protect it with reasonable safeguards and do not sell it, solicit your client, or share it without permission. I promptly report any known unauthorized access affecting your information. You own the report and delivered files. Appraisal Partners keeps a confidential workfile of its assistance for five years, or longer if required. Our nonpublic tools and process remain ours.

3. Price, changes, and revisions

The fixed fee in the assignment email does not change. If materially different work appears, I pause that added work and quote it before proceeding. Delivered work is invoiced monthly and due within 15 days. There is no subscription, minimum, or long-term commitment.

You may cancel before work starts at no charge. After work starts, I may invoice the completed portion, never more than the accepted fee. Corrections and revisions within the agreed assignment are included and take priority, including one extra comp each time a revision comes back. Additional comps or materially new work are quoted before they begin. If I cannot correct an item expressly included in the scope, that assignment's fee is waived.

4. Responsibility and liability

Appraisal Partners is responsible for its drafting and data entry against the sources provided or collected for the assignment. The signing appraiser remains responsible for the inspection, independent review, final analysis and conclusions, certification, signature, delivery, and professional obligations to the client and regulator.

Appraisal Partners' total liability for an assignment is limited to the greater of three times its accepted fee or $1,500. Neither side is liable to the other for consequential, incidental, or punitive damages or lost profits.

5. Ending the relationship and disputes

Either side may end the relationship by email. These terms still govern work already accepted.

Texas law governs. We first try to resolve any disagreement directly. An unresolved dispute goes to binding arbitration in Collin County, Texas, with small claims court available where applicable. If one provision cannot be enforced, the rest remain effective.

These terms, the agreement email, and each assignment email are the entire agreement. Changes require written acceptance by both sides, including by email. A later version applies only after it is accepted and does not change work already underway unless both sides agree.
