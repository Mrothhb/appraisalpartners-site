Production Desk Terms

Version dated August 27, 2026. Plain-language standard terms for direct appraiser relationships.

How these terms apply: they become binding only when incorporated into a written relationship schedule or accepted order. AMC and lender programs require their own vendor approval and written agreement unless that agreement expressly incorporates these terms. No production begins before written acceptance.

1. A separate service

These terms apply only to the Appraisal Partners Production Desk. The Production Desk is a distinct service line, with its own client relationships, scope, onboarding, agreement, and workflow. A residential appraisal performed and signed by Matthew Roth is a separate engagement under separate client terms.

2. The work

Appraisal Partners of Texas, Inc., operating the Appraisal Partners Production Desk (“we”), prepares editable draft appraisal reports and related support under the scope agreed during onboarding and in the accepted order. Matthew Roth holds Certified Residential credentials in Texas (#1361115-CR) and Colorado (#CR200002387). Depending on the agreed scope, work may include authorized data collection, public-record and MLS research, comparable candidates and proposed analyses, report data and narrative, maps and exhibits, source organization, and consistency and completion checks.

3. What the Production Desk does not become

We do not become the appraiser of record, accept the assignment from your client, inspect the subject, sign or deliver the appraisal, or give your client an opinion of value. This is not employment, staffing, a partnership, a co-appraisal, or a supervisor–trainee arrangement. Work is accepted only where the assignment scope, client terms, licensing, competence, and authorized data access permit it.

4. It stays your appraisal

You retain the client and engagement relationship, complete and document the inspection, independently review every draft, and accept, change, or reject anything in it. Scope, final comparable selection, final adjustments, analyses, reconciliation, opinion of value, certification, signature, and delivery remain yours. You must independently verify, adopt, revise, or reject the draft before signing.

5. Disclosure is not optional

When our work constitutes significant real property appraisal assistance, the certification identifies Matthew Roth and describes the assistance provided. Client, jurisdiction, and licensing requirements are confirmed before acceptance. You may edit the disclosure for accuracy; removing a required disclosure ends our work together.

6. Ownership and client relationships

You own the report, working file, and delivered support package. We keep a confidential record of our assistance. We do not solicit your clients, resell assignment information, or reuse the work for another customer.

7. Data and credentials

Research uses our own authorized access in the applicable market, or another written arrangement permitted by the data provider. Nobody shares MLS, software, portal, or client credentials in either direction. Before acceptance, you confirm that the engagement and client permit the requested named assistance.

8. Fees, capacity, and delivery

Fees, invoicing, capacity, exact deliverables, and committed delivery times are stated in the relationship schedule and/or accepted order. Nothing binds until approved in writing, and no undisclosed fee is added afterward. Changes in scope, new evidence, ROV work, or accelerated timing are agreed before the additional work begins. We correct our own drafting or entry errors without an additional production fee.

9. Insurance and status

We maintain applicable E&O coverage and current Certified Residential credentials in Texas and Colorado; evidence is available during onboarding. Either side may end the relationship in writing; accepted orders are completed and paid under their existing terms.

10. Responsibility and liability

Each side remains responsible for the work assigned to it. We answer for the accuracy of our entry and drafting against the information and sources available to us. You remain responsible for independently verifying and adopting the final analyses and conclusions, certification, signature, and obligations to your client and regulator. Our total liability on any order is limited to three times the fee for that order. Neither side is liable to the other for consequential, incidental, or punitive damages or lost profits. Services are provided as described in this agreement and the accepted scope.

11. Confidentiality

Assignment and borrower information remains confidential under the USPAP ETHICS RULE and applicable privacy law. Files are used only to perform the accepted work: no outside human subcontractors, nothing sold, and nothing reused for another customer. The relationship schedule identifies the software and service providers expected to handle assignment data and their available data-use and retention terms. We do not knowingly place assignment data in public tools or authorize it for training a model used by other customers. Materials beyond our assistance record are deleted on written request, subject to legal and professional recordkeeping obligations.

12. The legal basics

Texas law governs. If a disagreement arises, both sides first discuss it in good faith. Anything unresolved goes to binding arbitration in Collin County, Texas; small claims court remains available where applicable. If one clause fails, the rest remain effective. These terms, the relationship schedule, and accepted order emails form the entire agreement unless replaced in writing.
