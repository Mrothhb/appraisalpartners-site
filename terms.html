Version dated August 25, 2026. Plain language on purpose — this is the agreement, not a summary of one.

How this becomes binding: when we accept your order by email, that email references these terms. Reply ACCEPTED and this agreement is formed — for that order and every later one, until either side changes the terms in writing. No production begins before your ACCEPTED.

1. The work

Appraisal Partners ("we" — Matt Roth, TX Certified Residential #1361115-CR) drafts UAD 3.6 reports only — the dynamic URAR in full, hybrid, or desktop scope — from what you send: inspection results, photos, sketch, contract, and comps when you supply them. Data entry, comparable research support, drafting, and quality checks. That's real property appraisal assistance under USPAP — nothing more. No legacy forms, on any terms.

2. What we don't do

We don't sign, don't inspect, don't talk to your client, and don't give your client an opinion of value. We currently don't accept FHA, VA panel, or Georgia assignments. Full production from photos, sketch, and notes — where we pull comps from scratch — isn't part of this agreement; that's a separate written arrangement.

3. It stays your appraisal

You review every draft and accept, change, or reject anything in it before signing. Your signature means the work is yours, assisted as disclosed. Proposed adjustments come with their data basis, for your approval — nothing stands without your sign-off.

4. Disclosure isn't optional

Every report names Matt Roth and summarizes what we did, per USPAP Standards Rule 2-3. You may edit the disclosure's wording; removing it ends our work together.

5. You own it

The report, the working file, and the workfile copy are yours. We keep a confidential copy of our draft work as our own record, and that's all it's ever used for. We won't solicit your clients or resell anything from your assignments.

6. MLS

Research runs under your membership with us registered as your assistant (where your MLS allows), or on our own access in that market. Nobody shares logins, in either direction.

7. Money and turn times

Your first report is free — if you're not satisfied with it, you owe nothing. After that, every report is $89. Unusually complex assignments carry a surcharge quoted in writing at acceptance and approved by you in writing before we start — never after; rush is available case by case: the fee and the committed delivery time are quoted together in writing, scale with the deadline requested, and bind only once you approve the quote in writing — no rush commitment exists before that. Invoiced weekly; card or ACH from the invoice link, due within 7 days. Turn times run from acceptance — when the packet is confirmed complete, authorized, and assigned a production slot, with a delivery time quoted. Every accepted order is delivered within 24 hours of acceptance. The only exception is one we raise in advance: where undisclosed complexity, a technical failure, or a similar complication arises mid-assignment, we notify you before the deadline with a revised delivery time — never after it. Free work (the first report, and the First-File Conversion) runs in scheduled slots behind paid orders, typically 24–48 hours. Fixing our drafting mistakes is free, same cycle; revision requests touching our drafting are free; ROVs, new evidence, and scope changes are quoted first.

8. Insurance and status

We carry E&O at $1,000,000 per claim / $1,000,000 aggregate (certificate on request) and a current Texas Certified Residential credential. We're independent contractors — no partnership, no employment, no supervisor/trainee arrangement. Either side can end this in writing anytime; confirmed orders get finished and paid.

9. Responsibility and liability

We answer for the accuracy of our entry and drafting against what you sent. You answer for the appraisal — the analyses, the conclusions, the decision to sign, and everything you owe your client and your board. Our total liability on any order tops out at 3× the fee for that order, and neither side is liable to the other for consequential, incidental, or punitive damages or lost profits. Services are provided as described in this agreement — nothing else is promised or implied.

10. Confidentiality and your engagement

Assignment and borrower information stays confidential per the USPAP ETHICS RULE and privacy law; we disclose it only where law or USPAP requires. Before acceptance, you confirm that your engagement and client permit the requested assistance.

11. The legal basics

Texas law governs. If we ever disagree, we talk first, in good faith; what can't be resolved goes to binding arbitration in Collin County, Texas (small claims court stays available where it applies). If any clause fails, the rest stand. This page plus the accepted order emails are the entire agreement.
