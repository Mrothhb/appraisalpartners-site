      Before you ask

      What I’ll tell you up front.

      Is $149 the appraisal fee?
      No. It is what you pay me for the desk work on an appraisal you are billing your own client for.
      Who does the work?
      I do. I don’t farm your files out to anybody, and I don’t send them offshore.
      What if it comes back with conditions?
      Send them to me. Client or underwriter conditions tied to my work are handled for 30 days, no new fee. You are not troubleshooting a new form alone.
      Do you use AI?
      I use software automation and AI-assisted tools in this work, and yes, that includes the drafting. I check every report against its sources and read it through before it leaves.
      Is your help disclosed in the report?
      My assistance is named in the certification. That disclosure is not optional, and I do not remove it.
      Who signs it?
      You do. Accept, change, or reject anything in it. The final judgments, the signature and the delivery stay yours.
      What do you not take?
      No FHA or VA panel assignments, and no Georgia assignments under present desk policy. No legacy UAD 2.6/1004 production orders. This desk is UAD 3.6 only.
      What do you need from me?
      Your client has to permit outside assistance; you hold that engagement letter. No shared MLS, portal or software credentials, in either direction.
      Can I track the report?
      Yes. Every accepted report gets a private tracking link. See where it is at each step and choose email updates, text updates, both or status page only. See how it works.
