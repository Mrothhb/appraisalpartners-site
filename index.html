Two ways to run it — pick your lane

The standard order — start today, first file free. Data input: you send the whole packet — inspection results, notes, photos, sketch, your comps from MLS, public records, and any other data we can't or shouldn't pull on your behalf. We start and finish the report, up to sign.

Full appraisal — you inspect, you review, you sign; we research, collect, and write. Forward the order and engagement letter, then send photos, sketch, and notes when you have them — we do the rest: comps, market data, records, zoning, proposed adjustments and commentary, everything reaching you as a proposal for your review and signature. Limited availability right now, and case by case: we onboard first, make sure it's a good fit, and put it under a clear agreement before the first order. Details here.

How it works

  1. 1.Write us with the order basics; accepted orders get a secure upload link for the packet — inspection results, photos, sketch, your comps and data. (Onboarded for Full Research + Draft? Just the order, photos, sketch, and notes.) The desk's systems acknowledge instantly, and you get an answer within 30 minutes during desk hours: accepted with a quoted delivery time, what's missing, or a closer look. Never silence.
  2. 2.Grid built, adjustments proposed with their support for your approval, commentary drafted — next-day turnaround from acceptance. If anything ever threatens that clock, you hear from us before the deadline with a new committed time, never after it. Need it faster? Rush is quoted case by case — one written quote, fee and committed time together; nothing binds until you approve.
  3. 3.Review it. Change anything; it's your appraisal. Sign and deliver like always — our assistance is disclosed by name, the way USPAP requires.
