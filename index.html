Live work after the free trial

Your appraisal fee stays yours. You pay me for the desk work. The prices below are not appraisal fees and are not billed to an AMC, lender, borrower, or other end client. They are what the signing appraiser pays me for production work on an assignment the appraiser already controls and is billing for.

Why the desk fee works: before this desk, I spent about a decade as an engineer, much of it in product operations. I built the software- and AI-assisted production workflow for my own reports first. It removes most re-keying, routine form filling, document collection and organization, and clerical editing. The tools handle repetition, not appraisal decisions. My time stays on research, analysis, judgment, and personally reviewing every report; you keep every final appraisal decision. You have already won the assignment, handled the client, and inspected the property, so I price only the defined work behind you. Speed comes from better tooling, not lower standards or anonymous labor. These are the actual rates for the scopes shown, not introductory prices.

What comes back

An editable draft, not a printout. If you're on TOTAL, you get the native working file, ready to open in your own software with everything editable and the photos placed. On other software, you get the UAD 3.6 package: the XML, a draft-watermarked PDF, the exhibits, and the organized support file. Tell me your software and version at intake and I confirm compatibility at acceptance, not at delivery.

Every draft ships with a short list of open items worth your attention during review, the documented comp pool behind the grid, and suggested assistance-disclosure language. It arrives marked DRAFT and stays a draft until you've made it yours.

The line that never moves

  • Your client stays yours. I never solicit your clients.
  • You keep every final judgment: the comps, the adjustments, the opinion of value, the signature.
  • My assistance is named in the certification. That disclosure is not optional, and I do not remove it.
  • No shared MLS credentials, in either direction.
  • I don't farm your files out to anybody.
