The handoff

How a report moves

  1. 1.Send a short fit note. No property address or borrower information in the first message. You get an order number, a private status link, and a secure folder.
  2. 2.Send the packet. Order, photos, sketch, your notes, and the contract on a purchase. The start page lists exactly what your kind of assignment needs.
  3. 3.Get a written decision. I check permission, completeness, scope, software, and capacity. Production begins only after you confirm the scope, price, and return time.
  4. 4.Follow it, then finish it. Watch the private tracker or get milestone alerts by email or text. One consolidated direction round is included, and my production errors are corrected without another fee.
