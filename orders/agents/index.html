Meet your agents

Your portal comes with two AI assistants we call agents. They answer your questions, usually in seconds, any time of day.

Your Desk Agent

Find it: tap Desk Agent at the top of your portal.

Think of it as your assistant at our front desk. It knows your office and your orders.

  • Tells you where each order stands and when it's due.
  • Answers questions about your orders, reports and files.
  • Takes your requests, and passes anything that needs a person to Matt and our team.
  • Gets to know your office and how you like things done.

Your Report Agent

Find it: on your report's review page, tap Report Agent.

Each order gets its own Report Agent. It knows that report inside and out: the comparables, the adjustments and the comments.

  • Answers your questions while you review, right beside your report.
  • Explains any number, adjustment or comment in plain words.
  • Passes anything it can't answer to Matt and our team.

We're turning it on for more orders every week.

How it works

  1. Open your portal, or your report's review page.
  2. Tap the agent and type your question the way you'd say it to a person. On a phone, you can tap the microphone on your keyboard and just talk.
  3. Your agent answers right there, usually in seconds. If something needs a person, it tells you and passes it along.

Why we built it

Email is slow for quick questions. Something as simple as "Is my report done yet?" can take hours and several messages.

Your agents answer right away, day or night. Over time, your Desk Agent gets to know your office the way a good assistant does.

That gives Matt and our team more time for your reports, and less time on messages.

What it means for your workday

  • Answers in seconds, not hours.
  • Open any time: early mornings, evenings and weekends.
  • Everything in one place: your orders, due dates and reports.
  • Fewer emails to write, read and keep track of.
  • Quicker reviews: ask about your report while you're looking at it.
  • Less repeating yourself: your agent remembers how your office works.

Getting the most from your agents

  • Ask your agent first. Before you write an email or pick up the phone, try your Desk Agent.
  • Ask about a report on its review page. Your Report Agent is right there with you.
  • Tell it once. Share how your office likes things done, and it remembers.
  • Give it a week. Like any new habit, it feels natural after a few days.
  • Email still works. Your shared folder and desk@aandap.com work just like before. Use whatever is easiest.
