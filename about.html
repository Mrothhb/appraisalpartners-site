The people behind the desk

The appraisal comes before the form.

We’re Matt and Tim Roth. Two generations in appraisal, with one standard: understand the problem before opening the template.

Matt runs the desk.

Certified Residential · Texas & Colorado

I’m Matt Roth. I trained in Tim’s Southern California practice beginning in 2006, from trainee through licensure. I later ran M R Pacific Appraisals in Los Angeles and established Appraisal Partners in North Texas.

Twenty years and twelve metro markets in three states taught me to look past the template. Condition, location, market behavior and the details that move value matter more than a filled-in box.

My experience includes single-family homes, condos, PUDs, two-to-four units, new construction, acreage, view and waterfront properties, complex residential work, retrospective assignments and appraisal review. I know what the person signing is responsible for because I’ve carried it myself.

I run the desk day to day and personally review every report before it comes back. Your files stay in my hands.
